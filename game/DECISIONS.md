# Decisions for “Group Project”

Every decision about the game is listed here with its reason. The rules are the same as in the old [DECISIONS.md](../archive/DECISIONS.md) of “We Be(a)t”:

- Every decision gets a number (S-xx), a date, the decision itself and the reason.
- Entries are not deleted. If a decision is changed, a new entry is added, and the old one gets the note “replaced by S-xx”.
- Column “By”: **Team** means the team decided it. **Proposal** means Claude proposed it and the team has not confirmed it yet.

Some numbers are missing: S-169, S-174 to S-179, S-183, S-184, S-204 and S-209 were taken by branches that worked at the same time and never became an entry on `main`; no entry was deleted. German quotes from the team are given with an English translation.

As of 2026-11-23. On 2026-11-18 the team confirmed all entries except those with “Proposal” in the last column; later entries name who decided them.
